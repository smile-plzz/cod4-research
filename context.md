# Project Context & Scope Specification

## Project Goals
- Produce an exhaustive, publishable, interdisciplinary academic research monograph (9,000–11,000 words excluding references/appendices) and complete 20-file research portfolio on *Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare* (2007) and *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare Remastered* (2016).
- Reconstruct the full campaign narrative in chronological and mission order.
- Provide comprehensive character studies for all 13 major figures and supporting cast.
- Analyze all 21 single-player missions across 20 analytical dimensions.
- Contrast parallel playable perspectives (Soap, Jackson, young Price, Al-Fulani, AC-130 TV Operator, unnamed SAS operative).
- Deconstruct military technology, weapon handling, ballistics, vehicle mechanics, and combined-arms gameplay.
- Perform critical geopolitical and ideological analysis (Russian Ultranationalism, Middle Eastern coup depiction, Western intervention, Post-9/11 War on Terror discourse, neocolonialism/orientalism).
- Undertake side-by-side technical and aesthetic comparative analysis between the 2007 original and 2016 remaster.
- Evaluate the multiplayer ludic loop (Create-a-Class, killstreaks, prestige, map design) and its ideological juxtaposition with the campaign.

## Theoretical & Interdisciplinary Stack
- **Game Studies & Ludology:** Procedural rhetoric (Bogost), ludonarrative dissonance/resonance, agency and constraint, spatial storytelling (Jenkins).
- **Media & Cultural Studies:** Semiotics of realism, Roland Barthes' "Reality Effect" (*l'effet de réel*), remediation (Bolter & Grusin), audiovisual spectacle.
- **Military Studies & International Relations:** Militainment and the military-industrial-media-entertainment network (Der Derian, Lenoir & Caldwell, Payne, Robinson), virtuous war, remote lethal force, just war theory.
- **Postcolonial & Discourse Studies:** Representation of the "Other", Orientalism (Said), neocolonial discourse in digital games (Parlak & Kaygusuz, 2025).
