## Project Identification

- **Primary Discipline:** Game Studies
- **Secondary Disciplines:** Military Studies, Media Studies, Political Science, Narrative Studies, Cultural Studies, Psychology of Interactive Media, International Relations, Digital Humanities
- **Citation Format:** APA 7th Edition
- **Target Manuscript Length:** 9,000–11,000 words (excluding references and appendices)
- **Primary Research Object:** Complete single-player campaign of *Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare* (2007, Infinity Ward, Activision)
- **Comparative Research Object:** *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare Remastered* (2016, Raven Software, Activision)
- **Secondary Research Object:** Original and remastered multiplayer systems
