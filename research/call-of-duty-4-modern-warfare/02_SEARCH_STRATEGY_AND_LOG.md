# 02. Academic Search Strategy & Literature Screening Log

## 1. Information Retrieval Parameters & Strategy

### Target Databases and Repositories
- **Scholarly Databases:** Google Scholar, Crossref, OpenAlex, JSTOR, ScienceDirect / Scopus, Web of Science, DiGRA Digital Library, IEEE Xplore.
- **Specialized Journals in Game Studies & Military Media:** *Game Studies*, *Games and Culture*, *Journal of Integrated Studies*, *The RUSI Journal*, *Political Studies*, *International Journal of Politics, Culture, and Society*, *Critical Studies on Security*, *Media, War & Conflict*.
- **Primary Industry & Archival Repositories:** *Game Developer* (formerly Gamasutra), *Shacknews*, *GameSpot*, *Activision Developer Blog*, *PlayStation Blog*, *Eurogamer*, *Digital Trends*.

### Core Search Queries & Strings
1. `"Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare" AND ("game studies" OR "narrative" OR "procedural rhetoric")`
2. `("Call of Duty" OR "military shooter") AND ("militainment" OR "military-entertainment complex")`
3. `"Call of Duty 4" AND ("realism" OR "authenticity" OR "reality effect")`
4. `("Call of Duty" OR "first-person shooter") AND ("War on Terror" OR "post-9/11" OR "geopolitics")`
5. `"Call of Duty" AND ("neocolonialism" OR "orientalism" OR "Arab representation" OR "Russian representation")`
6. `("Death From Above" OR "AC-130") AND ("Call of Duty" OR "videogame" OR "remote warfare")`
7. `("All Ghillied Up" OR "Chernobyl") AND ("Call of Duty 4" OR "Infinity Ward" OR "stealth design")`
8. `("Shock and Awe" OR "Aftermath") AND ("Call of Duty 4" OR "nuclear" OR "player agency")`
9. `"Modern Warfare Remastered" AND ("Raven Software" OR "remaster" OR "visual fidelity" OR "preservation")`
10. `("Call of Duty 4" OR "Infinity Ward") AND ("postmortem" OR "making of" OR "interview")`

## 2. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

| Criterion | Inclusion Rules | Exclusion Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Topic Relevance** | Focuses directly on *Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare* (2007) or *Modern Warfare Remastered* (2016), or establishes fundamental theoretical frameworks for military shooters, militainment, procedural rhetoric, or Orientalism in digital media. | Casual game walkthroughs, cheat guides, unverified forum speculation, or generic video game reviews without analytical rigor. |
| **Object of Analysis** | Addresses the single-player campaign narrative, playable perspectives, mission scripting, gameplay mechanics, character arcs, multiplayer structure, or technical remastering. | Focus exclusively on later unrelated reboot entries (e.g., *Modern Warfare* 2019 or *Modern Warfare II* 2022) unless used explicitly for evolutionary comparison. |
| **Methodological Rigor** | Peer-reviewed journal articles, academic monographs, edited scholarly anthologies, conference proceedings, or authoritative primary developer postmortems and verified interviews. | Non-authoritative blog posts, clickbait gaming sites, AI-generated content farms, or unverified wikis without primary citations. |
| **Time Period** | Historical post-9/11 context (2001–2007) for initial release analysis; 2016–present for remaster evaluation and retrospective academic critiques. | Anachronistic projections treating events post-2007 as developer intent in 2007. |

## 4. Synthesis of Screening Outcomes
The retrieved corpus contains 27 peer-reviewed academic articles and scholarly books alongside 7 authoritative primary developer sources and game artifacts. This corpus provides full coverage of the project’s multidisciplinary scope: game studies mechanics (Bogost, Juul), cultural-military critiques (Payne, Der Derian, Lenoir & Caldwell, Robinson), ideological/postcolonial deconstruction (Parlak & Kaygusuz, Cao, Höglund, Šisler), spatial/cinematic aesthetics (Ramsay, Bos, Smethurst), and technical remastering documentation (Burnett, Pellas, Newman).
